Fresh-agent comparison: clean+REA, protected+REA and protected+Anti-BLA+REA agents each recovered all three newly randomized selected argument tasks and the benign control through static analysis. This establishes no incremental agent recovery win or general agent defeat. The controller verified194 checks and seven contained behavior variants after the answers; token/cost/peak-RSS fields remain unmeasured. Earlier parser/tool improvements retain their narrower scope.

Every claimed countermeasure needs its own protected-task evidence, normal controls and malformed controls. Expected capacity errors, projections, unsupported providers, parse admission and successful compilation are never counted as bypasses. Published REA distribution smokes are separate from the rebuilt pinned-source runtime.
